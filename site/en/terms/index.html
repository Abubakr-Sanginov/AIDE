The site
The public pages with the feature overview, documentation, changelog and installer.
The model

Installation and updates

The installer is distributed through the site and the GitHub releases page. Updates are installed over the previous version and do not remove your projects, settings or keys.

  • Update signature. Update packages are cryptographically signed. If the signature does not match, the app refuses to install the update — that is protection against swapped files.
  • Windows warning. The installer is not signed with a paid publisher certificate, so SmartScreen may ask for confirmation. It is not a sign of malicious code.
  • Versions. The author may release new versions, change the feature set and stop supporting older builds.

Your code and your data

The app claims no rights to your code. Everything you write, edit and generate in AIDE stays yours — including code the agent produced for your task.

Projects live on your disk. AIDE does not upload them to its own storage and keeps no cloud copies. What exactly leaves the machine when a model is called is described in the Privacy policy.

  • Model answers. Generated text can be wrong. The app shows it as a proposal, not as a fact, and you make the decision.
